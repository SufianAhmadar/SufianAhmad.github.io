🎮 Crafting Midcore-Friendly Mechanics

A huge task when designing advertising for midcore titles is simplifying mechanics for the user. This often means reworking and collaborating with dev teams to create clear narratives and easy-to-follow gameplay loops. At the same time, we must maintain ROAS by ensuring players recognize the game they saw in the ad when they land on the store. My approach focuses on evolving ad hooks with industry trends and player preferences while keeping core in-game assets front and center.

🧩 Bringing Puzzle Mechanics to Life

As a Unity developer, I transform simple puzzle mechanics into engaging, polished experiences. By combining smart level design with smooth interactions and subtle animations, I create gameplay that feels intuitive yet satisfying. My focus is on making every tap, swipe, and move feel rewarding — turning basic mechanics into memorable, player-first moments.

🎮 Prototyping Games with Top Publishers

I’ve collaborated with leading publishers like HOMA Games, Super Sonic, Lion Studios, and Rollic Games to develop rapid prototypes across multiple genres. This experience strengthened my skills in iteration, fast-paced development, and understanding what makes games marketable and fun.

🎨 Seasonal Creative Iterations

I design and iterate seasonal creatives across multiple games, aligning ad visuals with holidays, trends, and special events. This keeps campaigns fresh, improves engagement, and consistently boosts performance by adapting to players’ moods and cultural moments.
